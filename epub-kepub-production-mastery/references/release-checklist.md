# Release checklist

## Editorial

- The reader promise, audience, prerequisites, and supported environment are clear.
- Every planned topic has sufficient explanation and an appropriate example or practice task.
- Source coverage and omissions are recorded; repeated explanations have one canonical home.
- Chapters follow a coherent learning sequence, use a consistent heading hierarchy, and transition naturally.
- Changing technical behavior and historical details have been checked against primary sources.

## Files and navigation

- Standard EPUB 3 and any target-specific KePUB outputs are built from the same approved editorial source.
- Metadata, cover, manifest, spine, navigation, and language are correct.
- Local links, chapter links, references, and image paths resolve.
- Large books contain independently editable parts, and combined builds are assembled from approved parts only.
- A combined book has one global TOC and a linked quick reference; part and combined content reconcile.

## Reader checks

- Structural verification passes, and EPUBCheck is run when available.
- Kindle Previewer or the intended Amazon conversion path is reviewed for Kindle delivery.
- Kobo files are reviewed on a target Kobo device or app when Kobo delivery is required.
- Cover, ordinary text, a dense page, code, tables, images, large font, and dark mode receive visual review.
- Structural test results are not presented as visual or hardware acceptance.

## Delivery note

Report paths, formats, chapter and part counts, word counts, source coverage, material omissions, verification performed, device/renderers tested, and remaining review gates. State clearly when the work is a local study build or still needs publication-rights review.
